Privacy Policy

Application: Escrutar la Palabra (com.isaacbarnuevo.escrutar)
Developer: Isaac Barnuevo • Last updated: October 11, 2026
Core Commitment: «Escrutar la Palabra» operates entirely on your device (100% offline). We do not collect, store on external servers, or share any personal data from our users.

1. General Information

«Escrutar la Palabra» is a free Catholic scripture reading, study, and biblical scrutiny application based on Holy Scripture and cross-references. We deeply respect your privacy and guarantee that the application does not track its users.

2. Data We Collect

None. The application:

  • Does not require registration or account creation (does not ask for name, email address, phone number, or passwords).
  • Does not use cookies or advertising trackers, nor any third-party analytics or telemetry tools.
  • Does not access sensitive device permissions (camera, microphone, contacts, shared storage, or GPS location).

3. Local Storage on Your Device

All personalized features are stored exclusively in your device's internal local memory (using application web local storage):

  • Scrutiny trees, chains of connected passages, and saved journeys.
  • Personal notes, echoes, and reflections added to scripture verses.
  • Bookmarks and reading preferences (light, dark, or sepia mode, font family, and font size).

This data remains on your phone or tablet. The developer has no access to it at any time, nor is it transmitted to external servers. In accordance with standard operating system functions (such as Google Drive device backups configured by the user on their Google account), this local data may be included in the user's personal device backup if that feature is active. If you uninstall the application or clear its data from system settings, this information will be removed from your device.

4. External Links and Sharing Features

  • Sharing scrutinies: When you use the export or share button for a scrutiny, the application invokes the operating system's native share menu so you can choose where to send the text (for example, messaging apps, email, or clipboard).
  • Optional external links: The application contains an optional voluntary link to the Ko-fi platform (https://ko-fi.com/isaacbarnuevo) for users who wish to support the project. Tapping this link opens the device's external web browser under the privacy terms of that third-party website.

5. Children's Privacy

The application is suitable for all audiences and does not collect personal information from any person, including children.

6. Copyright and Biblical Text Attribution

Biblical text: Santa Biblia Libre para el Mundo (SBLM), translated by David Williams and Michael Paul Johnson (international language identifier spablm, indexed at eBible.org), comprising the 73 books of the Catholic biblical canon including the deuterocanonical books. The consulted reference source identifies this translation as public domain. The source and provenance of the text are expressly recognized for transparency and in acknowledgment of its translators and contributors.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

If any changes are made to this Privacy Policy in the future, the updated version will be published on this page with the revised date indicated.
