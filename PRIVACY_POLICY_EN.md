# Privacy Policy — Escrutar la Palabra

**Last updated:** October 11, 2026  
**Application:** Escrutar la Palabra (`com.isaacbarnuevo.escrutar`)  
**Developer:** Isaac Barnuevo

---

## 1. General Information

**«Escrutar la Palabra»** is a free scripture reading, study, and scriptural scrutiny application designed to operate entirely on the user's device (*100% offline*). We deeply respect your privacy: **this application does not collect, store on external servers, or share any personal data of its users.**

---

## 2. Data We Collect

**None.** The application:
- **Does not require registration or account creation** (does not ask for name, email address, phone number, or passwords).
- **Does not use cookies, advertising trackers**, or third-party analytics tools.
- **Does not access sensitive device permissions** (camera, microphone, GPS location, contacts, etc.).

---

## 3. Local Storage on Your Device

All personalized features of the application are stored **exclusively in your device's local internal memory** (using the application's local storage):
- **Saved scrutiny trees and reading trails.**
- **Personal notes, echoes, and verse reflections.**
- **Reading bookmarks and preferences** (light/dark/sepia mode, font family, and font size).

This data remains on your phone or tablet. The developer has no access to it at any time, nor is it transmitted to developer servers. In accordance with standard operating system functions (such as Google Drive device backups configured by the user on their Google account), this local data may be included in the user's personal device backup if that feature is active. If you uninstall the application or clear its data from Android system settings, this information will be removed from your device.

---

## 4. External Links and Sharing Features

- **Sharing scrutinies:** When you use the export or share button for a scrutiny, the application invokes the operating system's native share menu so you can choose where to send the text (for example, messaging apps, email, or clipboard).
- **Optional external links:** The application contains an optional voluntary link to the *Ko-fi* platform (`https://ko-fi.com/isaacbarnuevo`) for users who wish to support the developer. Tapping this link opens the device's external web browser under the privacy terms of that third-party website.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

The application is suitable for all audiences and does not collect personal information from any person, including children.
